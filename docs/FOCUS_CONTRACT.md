## Decision architecture

One hero: *who you are / what needs deciding / current legal action / one CTA*. Optional `Altre strade` provides up to three labeled plans and their uncertainties. Below the fold: one compressed abstract scene, optional world exploration and optional story/ledger. The advanced game remains a separate mode explicitly starting a new session, because state transfer between clients is not implemented. Focus Mode is **the default** `/web/index.html` entry.

## Limits

Automated scenario variants do not establish actual comprehension, response times, stress or fun. They prove specified invariants. The persistent 24/7 server, complete 16-quarter campaign, 20-player validation and WCAG certification remain future gates. Map selection is a lens, not an invented geographical outcome.
