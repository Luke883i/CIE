# CIE F6: Reticular V9 + Cross-book Economic Integration
Ruleset: `CIE-F6-1.0.0`, fictional-game model. Any V9 interpretation is a hypothesis or synthetic assumption, not a causal estimate.

## Runtime contract
F6 wraps immutable-authority F4/F5 episode logic and adds a source-linked game-policy intervention each economic quarter. The V9 registry contains exactly the 1,037 V9 source units (20 V4 + 1,000 V6 + 12 V7 + 5 V8 per source manifest), with `uid` and source text. **There are exactly five domain archetypes, not 1,037 independently reviewed causal models.** Each selected case runs one bounded T0–T5 cascade with two explicit feedback links (T3→T1, T4→T0); the typed cascade is a game mechanic only.

## Ledger contract
F3/F4 historical ledger sums to 160. F5 opening economic ledger sums to 440. F6 establishes a *single integrated world portfolio* by a one-time documented 8-unit transfer between the legacy Treasury and the economy Treasury. Historical F4 records remain immutable; F6 current-view legacy Treasury is reduced by 8 while the economic Treasury receives 8, with one bridge journal entry. At every F6 economic tick: legacy current 152 + economic current 448 = **600** conserved. F5 journal reconciles against a modified opening balance of 448; F4 journal reconciles to its historical 160 snapshot. This is an explicit interbook bridge, but **not yet a stock-flow-consistent Eurozone macroeconomy** (F11/G06 remains open).

## User choices and hard gates
Each quarter offers Investigate (+resilience, +legitimacy, temporary -production), Cooperate (+support and capacity with conditional risk) or Accelerate (+production and short-term support with risk and review). Governance-domain acceleration is barred by non-compensatory rights/competence gate. Financial and institutional decisions still follow F5's capabilities; F6 never invents powers for another role. Player sees the dossier mechanism, intervention, falsifier, rights proviso and the list of T0–T5 effects. Fast-forward remains explicit, bounded 1–4 quarters and critical-event stoppable.

## Falsifiers
Invalid UID, duplicate bridge, change to the prior judicial act or historical cash, disallowed acceleration in governance, unauthorized economic choice, non-conserved crossbook sum, wrong replay digest, unstated source proof status, missing T-stage, repeated case ID with same seed causing divergent results, and denied private role data are hard failures.

## Limits
Browser-owner can inspect local model state; V9's provenance is not proof of empirical effect. The source registry has 1,037 registered cases, **37 curated source cases** and 1,000 template cases; runtime execution uses only five synthetic domain rule families. Deep semantic case-by-case validity, independent legal analysis, macro SFC, balance/playtesting, persistent server, security certification and shipping are not supported by F6.
